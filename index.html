Three things this data cannot yet answer

Each is a missing column, not a missing analysis. Add them at capture and these views build themselves next month.

  1. Repayment delays. No due date or instalment number exists anywhere in the source, so adherence to an EMI schedule cannot be measured. Add EMI Due Date and EMI Instalment No to the capture sheet.
  2. True discounting. No list price is recorded, so a real discount percentage is impossible. The workbook proxies it against each course's own median. Send the official price list and it converts to genuine discount tracking.
  3. Outstanding balances. The Outstanding column arrived completely empty. Figures here are derived as agreed minus received on the sale row, which overstates the balance wherever a later instalment was recorded separately.
Also worth fixing at source: seven rows close before their lead was created, 315 invoice numbers cover 331 rows, and several customer names hold a pasted GST address block instead of a name.
